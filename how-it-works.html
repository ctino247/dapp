How It Works

Overview

Social Circle is a social contribution platform built on blockchain technology. It allows participants to join a shared contribution pool under predefined, transparent rules. Participation is voluntary and fully on-chain.

Social Circle does not offer investment services, profit guarantees, or financial advice.

Step-by-Step Process

1. Connect Wallet

Users connect a supported crypto wallet to access the platform. The wallet acts as the user’s identity within Social Circle.

No personal account or registration is required.

2. Join the Social Circle

To participate, a user submits a contribution transaction that meets the platform’s defined requirements.

Once the transaction is confirmed on the blockchain:

  • The user is marked as an active participant
  • The contribution is processed automatically by the protocol

3. On-Chain Processing

All contributions are handled by a smart contract deployed on the blockchain. This means no manual intervention, no hidden processes, and no off-chain manipulation. Every transaction is publicly verifiable.

4. Transparency & Verification

Users can view their own transaction history, protocol activity, and contract interactions. All data is sourced directly from the blockchain.

Important Notes

  • Participation does not guarantee any outcome
  • Contributions may not be reversible
  • Users are responsible for their own wallet security
